Guidelines

Membership rules

Clear rules ensure fairness and accountability for every member of Zarafi.

💵

Weekly savings range

Every member must contribute between $50 and $300 per week. Contributions are expected mainly on weekends.

⚠️

Missed contribution penalty

Members who miss a weekly contribution will be flagged and a $50 penalty will be applied to their account.

📊

Loan limit: 3× savings

The maximum loan a member can take is three times their total savings. Guarantor savings also factor in.

🤝

Guarantor requirement

All loan applications must include guarantors. If a borrower defaults, their guarantors are held responsible.

📅

Repayment schedule

Repayment terms and installment amounts are defined at approval. Members must adhere to the schedule.

🔍

Full transparency

All transactions — savings, loans, penalties, repayments — are recorded and visible to each member.

How eligibility works

Your savings × 3
= Maximum loan amount

Eligibility is calculated from your total savings plus your guarantors' combined savings — which must cover the loan requested.

Example: With $1,000 saved, you can borrow up to $3,000 — provided your guarantors meet the savings threshold too.

Members see their full repayment schedule — due dates, installment amounts, and outstanding balance — directly in their dashboard.
